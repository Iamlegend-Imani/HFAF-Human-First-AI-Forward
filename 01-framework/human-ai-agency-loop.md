# The Human–AI Agency Loop

The Human–AI Agency Loop is a five-stage model for using AI to expand capability while preserving human agency.

## 1. Human Intent

Begin with the human, not the tool.

Ask: **What am I trying to understand, decide, create, or accomplish?**

AI should not determine purpose by default. Human intention establishes the objective, stakes, and definition of success.

## 2. Context

Give the system enough of the world surrounding the problem to collaborate meaningfully.

Context can include prior thinking, evidence, lived experience, constraints, uncertainties, audience, disciplinary knowledge, values, voice, and competing considerations.

Context is not merely a prompting technique. Articulating context can force the human to clarify their own thinking.

## 3. Collaboration

Use AI where augmentation creates meaningful leverage.

Possible roles include research assistant, tutor, critic, simulator, editor, brainstorming partner, translator, coding assistant, synthesis engine, analyst, or question generator.

The question is not simply **Can AI do this?**

Ask instead: **What division of cognitive labor best serves the human objective?**

## 4. Discernment

Interrogate the output.

- Is it true?
- What evidence supports it?
- What is missing?
- What assumptions were introduced?
- Could bias be affecting the result?
- What alternative interpretation exists?
- Does this actually answer the question?
- Can I defend it without appealing to the AI as authority?

AI literacy requires knowing when not to trust an answer.

## 5. Human Authorship

Return decision rights to the human.

Ask:

- What do I believe?
- What will I keep, change, or reject?
- What am I willing to defend?
- What action will I take?
- What am I accountable for?

The final artifact may be AI-assisted. Responsibility for it remains human.

## The loop

**Human Intent → Context → AI Collaboration → Human Discernment → Human Authorship → Reflection → renewed Human Intent**

This is a loop rather than a pipeline because outcomes generate new information. Humans reflect, update their understanding, and begin another cycle with richer context.

> **AI should reduce the distance between human intention and human execution without reducing human agency.**

# Human–AI Use Spectrum

AI use does not have to be reduced to a binary choice between **allowed** and **prohibited**.

The appropriate mode depends on the human capability, task, risk, and learning objective.

| Mode | Role of AI | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Only** | No AI participation | Closed assessment, personal reflection, foundational skill demonstration |
| **AI Consulted** | AI expands possibilities | Brainstorm questions or explore alternative perspectives |
| **AI Assisted** | AI supports portions of execution | Editing, coding assistance, organization, analysis |
| **AI Collaborative** | Human and AI iterate together | Complex problem solving, scenario development, iterative research |
| **AI Evaluated** | AI output becomes material for human critique | Fact-check, identify bias, critique reasoning, improve an AI response |
| **AI Designed** | Human designs systems or workflows involving AI | Build an agent, workflow, evaluation protocol, or AI-enabled tool |
| **Human Verified** | Human must independently demonstrate understanding and accountability | Oral defense, demonstration, source verification, reflective explanation |

## Design principle

Do not begin with: **Is AI allowed?**

Begin with: **What human capability are we trying to develop or preserve?**

Then determine the appropriate role for AI.

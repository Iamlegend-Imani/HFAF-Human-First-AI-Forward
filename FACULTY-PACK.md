## The seven questions

For any AI-mediated assignment, activity, or workflow, ask:

1. **Human outcome:** What human capability are we trying to develop?
2. **AI role:** What may AI appropriately assist with?
3. **Human-only zone:** What should not be delegated in this context?
4. **Verification:** How will learners demonstrate understanding?
5. **Disclosure:** What AI use should be documented?
6. **Risk:** What privacy, bias, accuracy, equity, or integrity concerns exist?
7. **Reflection:** What did AI improve, and what might it have diminished?

## Facilitation principle

The purpose is not to convince every faculty member to maximize AI use. The purpose is to make AI use **intentional, legible, pedagogically defensible, and accountable to human learning outcomes**.

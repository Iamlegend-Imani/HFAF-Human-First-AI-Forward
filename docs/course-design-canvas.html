Human–AI Course Design Canvas

Use this when designing or redesigning an assignment, course activity, assessment, or learning experience.

1. Human Outcome

What human capability are we developing?

2. Evidence of Understanding

How will we know the learner actually possesses that capability?

3. Human-Only Zone

Which cognitive or experiential work should not be delegated to AI? Why?

4. AI Role

Choose deliberately: Human Only, AI Consulted, AI Assisted, AI Collaborative, AI Evaluated, AI Designed, Human Verified. What exactly may AI do?

5. Verification

What must the learner independently verify: sources, claims, calculations, reasoning, quotations, assumptions, code, interpretations, or other outputs?

6. Disclosure

How should AI use be documented?

7. Human Authorship

How will the learner demonstrate what they accepted, rejected, changed, concluded, and believe?

8. Risks

Consider factual error, bias, privacy, academic integrity, dependency, deskilling, inequitable access, and discipline-specific ethical concerns.

9. Reflection

What did AI improve? What did it diminish? What required the most human judgment? What should be done differently next time?

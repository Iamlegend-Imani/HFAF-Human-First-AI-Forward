Human–AI Use Spectrum

AI use does not have to be reduced to a binary choice between allowed and prohibited. The appropriate mode depends on the human capability, task, risk, and learning objective.

ModeRole of AIExample
Human OnlyNo AI participationClosed assessment, personal reflection, foundational skill demonstration
AI ConsultedAI expands possibilitiesBrainstorm questions or explore alternative perspectives
AI AssistedAI supports portions of executionEditing, coding assistance, organization, analysis
AI CollaborativeHuman and AI iterate togetherComplex problem solving, scenario development, iterative research
AI EvaluatedAI output becomes material for human critiqueFact-checking, bias identification, reasoning critique, response improvement
AI DesignedHuman designs systems or workflows involving AIBuild an agent, workflow, evaluation protocol, or AI-enabled tool
Human VerifiedHuman independently demonstrates understanding and accountabilityOral defense, demonstration, source verification, reflective explanation
Do not begin with: “Is AI allowed?” Begin with: “What human capability are we trying to develop or preserve?”

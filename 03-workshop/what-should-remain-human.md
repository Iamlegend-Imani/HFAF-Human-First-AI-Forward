# What Should Remain Human?

## A Human First, AI Forward faculty workshop

**Suggested length:** 60–90 minutes

## Purpose

Move the faculty conversation beyond **ban AI / embrace AI** and toward intentional decisions about human capability, augmentation, assessment, and accountability.

## Outcomes

Participants should leave able to:

- identify the human capability underneath an assignment;
- distinguish augmentation from substitution;
- choose an appropriate AI-use mode;
- redesign one existing assignment;
- establish verification and disclosure expectations;
- articulate what must remain human in their discipline.

## Workshop flow

### 1. Opening question — 10 minutes

Ask participants:

**What are you afraid humans will lose if AI becomes deeply integrated into education?**

Capture concerns without immediately debating them.

Then ask:

**What could humans gain?**

### 2. Reframe — 10 minutes

Introduce the core proposition:

> **Technology should increase human capacity without decreasing human agency.**

Introduce the Human–AI Agency Loop and the Human–AI Use Spectrum.

### 3. Assignment autopsy — 15 minutes

Each participant selects one real assignment and answers:

- What capability is this actually teaching?
- What part currently demonstrates understanding?
- Could AI produce the artifact without developing that capability?
- What should remain human?

### 4. Redesign — 20 minutes

Participants redesign the assignment using:

- Human outcome
- Human-only zone
- AI role
- Verification requirement
- Disclosure requirement
- Demonstration of understanding

### 5. Adversarial test — 10 minutes

Pair participants. One tries to identify how AI could circumvent the intended learning. The other strengthens the assignment.

### 6. Disciplinary boundary — 10 minutes

Complete the sentence:

**In my discipline, humans must remain responsible for __________ because __________.**

### 7. Closing — 5 minutes

Ask:

**What is one thing you will prohibit, one thing you will permit, and one thing you will deliberately teach students to do with AI?**

## Facilitation stance

This workshop is not designed to convert skeptics into AI enthusiasts.

Skepticism can be useful. The objective is to replace reflexive acceptance or rejection with informed human agency.

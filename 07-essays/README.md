# Human First, AI Forward Essays

Long-form public writing translating the framework into accessible arguments.

## Planned essay series

1. **The Human Remains** — AI should shorten the distance between thought and action, not remove the human in between.
2. **What Is Education For When AI Can Produce the Answer?** — Why generative AI forces us to reconsider what assessment actually measures.
3. **Delegate Execution. Never Delegate Agency.** — A practical boundary for cognitive augmentation.
4. **Adapt Without Disappearing** — Why technological adaptation and preservation of human capability are not opposing goals.
5. **AI as Ally** — Understanding AI as an external cognitive extension rather than a substitute for mind.
6. **Nothing Exists in Isolation** — Technology, behavior, cognition, health, capacity, creativity, and contribution as an interconnected system.

These essays may be adapted for public platforms while the repository remains the canonical home of the evolving framework.
